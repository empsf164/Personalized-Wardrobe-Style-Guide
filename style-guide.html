Styling Principles

Your Personal Styling Guidance

Curated Principles
  • Keep your base palette strictly neutral: Anchor with two neutrals (e.g. Ivory & Espresso) and introduce only one accent color (e.g. Muted Olive).
  • Balance relaxed silhouettes with structure: If wearing wide-leg trousers, pair with a clean tuck or slim knit; if wearing an oversized coat, keep trousers straight.
  • Invest in tactile natural fibers: Prioritize double-faced wool, raw silk, brushed cashmere, and organic linen over synthetic blends.
  • Let garment architecture speak: Rely on seam lines, lapel cuts, and clean proportions rather than loud logos or fleeting fast-fashion prints.
